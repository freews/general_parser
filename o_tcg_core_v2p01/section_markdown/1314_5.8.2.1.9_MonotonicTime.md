###### 5.8.2.1.9 MonotonicTime

> **Section ID**: 5.8.2.1.9 | **Page**: 292-292

This is the value of the monotonic counter when the log entry was created, as defined in section 5.5.
Note that if the Clock Template was not issued into this SP then this value SHALL be 0.
This column SHALL NOT be modifiable by the host.
