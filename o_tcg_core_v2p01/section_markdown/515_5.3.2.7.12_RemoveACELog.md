###### 5.3.2.7.12 RemoveACELog

> **Section ID**: 5.3.2.7.12 | **Page**: 163-163

This column identifies the conditions under which logging of the RemoveACE method invocation on this
access control association occurs.  This column SHALL be disregarded if the Log Template has not
been issued into the SP, and SHOULD be set to 0.
