##### 5.1.3.1 AC_element

> **Section ID**: 5.1.3.1 | **Page**: 100-100

An AC_element is a list type made up of ACE_expressions. The size of the AC_element list is
implementation-dependant.  A minimum size restriction MAY be defined by an SSC.
