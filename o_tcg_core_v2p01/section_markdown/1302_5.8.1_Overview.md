#### 5.8.1 Overview

> **Section ID**: 5.8.1 | **Page**: 291-291

An issued SP that incorporates the Log Template SHOULD incorporate the Clock Template to exploit
the full capabilities of logging.  See Section 5.5 for details on the Clock Template.
