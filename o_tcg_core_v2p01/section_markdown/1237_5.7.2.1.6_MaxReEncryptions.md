### 5.7.2.1.6 MaxReEncryptions

> **Section ID**: 5.7.2.1.6 | **Page**: 272-273

Revision 1.00
This value defines the maximum number of simultaneous re-encryption operations supported.
Simultaneous re-encryptions refer to the number of different LBA ranges that MAY be concurrently re-
encrypted. A value of 0 indicates Re-encryption is not supported.
This column SHALL NOT be modifiable by the host.
