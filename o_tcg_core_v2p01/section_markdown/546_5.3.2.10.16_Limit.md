###### 5.3.2.10.16 Limit

> **Section ID**: 5.3.2.10.16 | **Page**: 169-169

The Limit column defines a limit on the number of times that an authority MAY be authenticated, either
explicitly or implicitly.  This value represents the maximum number of total successful authentications
with this authority, including session start-up invocations and Authenticate method invocations.  A
value of 0 SHALL mean no limit.
