#### 2.2.1 Multicomponent Trusted Platform (MCTP)

> **Section ID**: 2.2.1 | **Page**: 23-23

Begin Informative Content
A Multicomponent Trusted Platform (MCTP) describes a platform in which one or more hosts,
applications, peripherals, or devices participate in the trust state.  In an MCTP, various host applications
communicate with the TPer through a peripheral interface such as ATA or SCSI.
End Informative Content
