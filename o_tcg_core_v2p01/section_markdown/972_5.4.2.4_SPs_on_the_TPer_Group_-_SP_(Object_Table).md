##### 5.4.2.4 SPs on the TPer Group - SP (Object Table)

> **Section ID**: 5.4.2.4 | **Page**: 232-233

The table in this section describes the data that the Admin SP keeps about all of the SPs on the TPer.
The Admin SP SHALL be UID=0x00 0x00 0x02 0x05 0x00 0x00 0x00 0x01 in the SP Table, and the
values in this table SHALL be readable by the Anybody authority.
